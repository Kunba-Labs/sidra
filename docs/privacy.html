Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. Sidra is made by Kunba Labs BV in the Netherlands.

We collect no data

Sidra has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Kunba Labs BV runs no server for Sidra and receives nothing from the app.

Connections the app makes

  • The SSH hosts, herdr machines, Theasa servers and GitLab servers that you add. Sidra connects to them while it runs.
  • Hacker News (its Firebase API and Algolia search) when you open HackerTab, and the icons of the sites it links to.

Those services handle your data under their own policies.

Data on your device

Host names, settings and your workspace layout are stored on your device. Passwords, tokens and SSH keys are stored in the Keychain. Keys that you make in the Secure Enclave never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this data.
